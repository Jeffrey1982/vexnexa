# VexNexa Product Context

## Register

Brand for the public marketing site, product for the authenticated dashboard, admin, scanner, and report workflows.

## Product Purpose

VexNexa helps agencies, accessibility consultants, and EU-facing teams find WCAG issues, monitor regressions, and deliver audit-ready accessibility reports. The product should feel like serious compliance infrastructure rather than a generic website scanner.

## Primary Users

Digital agencies that need white-label accessibility reports for clients.

Compliance, product, and web teams responsible for WCAG and European Accessibility Act readiness.

Consultants who need repeatable scans, evidence, and exportable findings.

## Brand Voice

Precise, calm, and credible. The product can be technical, but it should not feel cold or obscure. Copy should prefer proof, outcomes, and audit language over hype.

## Strategic Principles

Lead with evidence: screenshots, scores, report previews, severity counts, and audit artifacts should appear before abstract claims.

Make compliance feel manageable: show clear next actions, not fear-heavy legal language.

Keep trust visible: EU readiness, WCAG 2.2, white-label delivery, and continuous monitoring are core proof points.

Use restraint with personality: the interface should feel premium and distinctive, but never decorative at the cost of clarity.

## Anti-References

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages with centered hero stacks, repeated icon-card grids, and vague productivity claims.

Avoid dark cyber dashboards as the default look. Accessibility compliance should feel legible, inspectable, and trustworthy.

Avoid monochrome green-only styling. Emerald is the trust color, not the entire visual language.
