# VexNexa Game Studio

## Register
Brand: public independent game studio website. No authenticated product dashboard.

## Purpose and users
VexNexa presents its games to players, parents, press and prospective collaborators. The first featured product is IpiWow, an educational iPhone game. The primary action is to discover IpiWow and visit its real App Store listing. Secondary actions are learning about the studio and contacting the team.

## Confirmed direction
On 24 September 2026 Jeffrey approved the cyan/mint/ivory studio mockup with large condensed navy typography, two actual IpiWow app screens and the existing globe mascot. This approval supersedes the archived accessibility SaaS brief. The implementation must preserve these main visual ingredients with semantic, responsive UI, not a rasterized page.

## Brand voice
Sunny, curious, tangible. Direct, friendly language without invented reviews, awards, download figures, teams or release promises. IpiWow facts: iPhone, 10 knowledge categories, 200 levels, offline play, no ads or subscriptions. Native website content in Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish, European Portuguese and Tagalog.

## Technical principles
Keep the site largely static and inexpensive to operate on the existing Vercel project. A server-only Resend contact endpoint is the sole business integration. No Supabase, Prisma, Mollie, analytics, newsletters, account system or scheduled SaaS tasks in the active application. Preserve old source for rollback without routing or compiling it into the studio site.

## Anti-goals
No generic SaaS feature grids, pricing subscriptions, neon-on-black gaming template, fake game imagery, fabricated social proof, or contact success before the mail provider accepts a message.
